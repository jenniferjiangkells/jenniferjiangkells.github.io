A first thought, on how this site is put together.

It's a static site built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and hosted on GitHub Pages. The type is [Nanum Myeongjo](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nanum+Myeongjo) for body text, [Junicode 2](https://github.com/psb1558/Junicode-font) for the italic display lettering, and Berkeley Mono for code. The palette is warm paper and brick red with a teal accent — and there's a light/dark toggle hiding in the clock on the homepage.

The design is heavily influenced by [matthew j lee's personal website](https://matthewjl.xyz/). Funnily, I came across it IRL, at the [an exhibition on digital folklore and web craft at the Photographer's Gallery](https://thephotographersgallery.org.uk/whats-on/connection-established-digital-folklore-and-web-craft). I liked the vibe and took a picture of it. Then I gave it to Claude code and asked it to do its thing. Thanks Matthew and [LJ](https://ljvmiranda921.github.io/) (whose site I originally forked from)!

I swapped the Gingko leaves for a California poppy. I recently visited San Francisco and had never seen yellow poppies before. I love how bright and happy it feels, in contrast with the typical red poppies associated with Remembrance Day in the UK, which reminds me of cold miserable November days.
